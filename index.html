ADVANTAGES OF TECHNOLOGY

Technology makes engineering faster, safer, more accurate and more scalable

KEY BENEFITS

  1. Higher accuracy through simulation, sensing and digital measurement
  2. Faster design cycles using CAD, modelling and AI-assisted tools
  3. Improved safety through automation, monitoring and predictive maintenance
  4. Lower operating costs through optimization and smart manufacturing
  5. Better sustainability through renewable energy and resource efficiency
  6. Global collaboration through cloud platforms and connected systems

DISADVANTAGES & CHALLENGES

Engineering technology also creates new technical, economic and social responsibilities

KEY CHALLENGES

  1. High initial investment in advanced equipment and digital infrastructure
  2. Cybersecurity and data-privacy risks in connected systems
  3. Dependence on software, networks and reliable digital infrastructure
  4. Workforce disruption when automation replaces repetitive tasks
  5. Electronic waste and energy consumption from growing digital systems
  6. Ethical risks when AI decisions are poorly designed or supervised
